# Arduino LED Blink Demo

A minimal Arduino sketch that blinks an LED connected to digital pin 12.

## Hardware

- Arduino-compatible board
- LED
- 220-330 ohm resistor
- Jumper wires

## Wiring

Connect digital pin 12 to the LED's anode (long leg) through the resistor. Connect the LED's cathode (short leg) to GND.

## Upload

1. Open `HEMASRI_blink_1_led.ino` in the Arduino IDE.
2. Select your board and its port from the **Tools** menu.
3. Click **Upload**.

The LED turns on for one second and off for one second repeatedly.
